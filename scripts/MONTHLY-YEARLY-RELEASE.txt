CODE
- Offer com.ai.sofa.subscriptions.month and com.ai.sofa.subscriptions.year.
- Select annual by default; show annual price first, monthly equivalent second.
- Show 3 free days only when the monthly StoreKit product has a 3-day free
  introductory offer and StoreKit confirms the customer's eligibility.
- Otherwise show the monthly price without a trial claim.
- Remove the trial toggle: the billing period changes only via plan cards.
- Calculate Gift discount from actual comparable StoreKit prices. Hide the
  percentage and reference price when either price is missing, currencies or
  subscription periods differ, or Gift does not cost less. No fallback 30%.
- Keep Gift purchasing and entitlement/restoration code unchanged. Legacy
  weekly product support remains; do not remove that product from sale here.
- Prices in production come from StoreKit, never from the local test fixture.

APP STORE CONNECT — REQUIRED BEFORE ROLLOUT
- Monthly product: com.ai.sofa.subscriptions.month (existing approved product).
- US regular price: USD 4.99 per month.
- Configure a free introductory offer: 3 days, followed by regular monthly
  renewal. Check intended territories and actual effective date in Connect.
- Keep standard annual USD 39.99/year and Gift USD 29.99/year in the US.
- Other storefronts use their own localized prices; do not hardcode USD.
- This Git change DOES NOT create a live introductory offer in Connect.
- Do not automatically migrate, cancel or reprice existing weekly subscribers.

   Run scripts/paywall-smoke.swift with the Swift compiler command in its
   header (runtime model tests with empty/failing stores, no real purchases).
   Run scripts/paywall-regressions.swift with the command in its header

4. Restore an active legacy weekly subscription and confirm PRO access.
5. Verify Gift still uses year.gift, no free trial, actual localized discount.
6. Inspect paywall on small/large phones, English/Russian and long locales,

8. Compare paywall-to-paid conversion, trial-to-paid conversion, refunds,
   monthly renewal and net revenue per install. Do not judge by trials alone.
